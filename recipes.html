01 / CLASSIC

Classic
Carbonara

Ingredients

  • 200 g spaghetti
  • 100 g pancetta
  • 2 eggs
  • 50 g Pecorino Romano
  • Black pepper
  • Salt

Method

  1. Cook the pasta until al dente.
  2. Cook the pancetta until crispy.
  3. Mix eggs with Pecorino Romano.
  4. Combine the hot pasta with the pancetta.
  5. Remove from heat and add the egg mixture.
  6. Stir until creamy and serve immediately.

02 / CLASSIC

Margherita
Pizza

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough
  • Tomato sauce
  • Fresh mozzarella
  • Fresh basil
  • Olive oil
  • Salt

Method

  1. Prepare the dough.
  2. Spread tomato sauce.
  3. Add mozzarella.
  4. Bake until golden.
  5. Finish with basil and olive oil.
